SalaryToHourlyRate

Free, simple pay calculators that run in your browser.

SalaryToHourlyRate offers free calculators for working out what your pay adds up to. Every calculation runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter stay on your device.

The formulas are shown on each page so you can check the math. Results are estimates before tax. Real pay depends on your employer, location and benefits.
