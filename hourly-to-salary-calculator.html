How to convert hourly pay to a yearly salary

Multiply your hourly rate by your hours per week and by the weeks you work in a year. Overtime hours are paid at 1.5 times your rate and are added on top.

Yearly pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year

A standard full-time year is 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours.

Questions

Does this include tax? No. Results are gross pay, before income tax and other deductions.

How is overtime counted? Overtime hours are paid at 1.5 times your hourly rate and added to your regular weekly pay.

What if I take unpaid leave? Lower the weeks per year to match the weeks you are paid.
