About This Calculator

This tool helps you convert between annual, monthly, weekly and hourly wages. Results are estimates and don't include taxes, benefits, or deductions.

Salary to Hourly Conversion

Use this salary to hourly calculator to convert annual, monthly, weekly, or daily pay into an equivalent hourly wage. Enter your pay amount, choose the pay period, and adjust your weekly hours and paid weeks to match your work schedule.

The calculator shows gross pay before taxes and deductions. A standard full-time example uses 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks per year, but you can change those assumptions.

How to convert salary to hourly pay

Divide your yearly gross salary by the total paid working hours in a year. A common full-time assumption is 40 hours a week for 52 paid weeks, which equals 2,080 hours.

Hourly pay = yearly salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert salary to hourly pay?

Divide your annual gross salary by your total paid working hours in a year. For a 40-hour week and 52 paid weeks, that is 2,080 hours.

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

$50,000 a year is about $24.04 per hour when working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

How much is $60,000 a year per hour?

$60,000 a year is about $28.85 per hour when working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

How much is $75,000 a year per hour?

$75,000 a year is about $36.06 per hour when working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

How much is $100,000 a year per hour?

$100,000 a year is about $48.08 per hour when working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

How many working hours are in a year?

A common full-time assumption is 2,080 hours: 40 hours per week × 52 weeks. Your actual paid hours may be different.

Does the calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator shows gross pay before income tax, benefits, insurance, retirement contributions, and other deductions.

How does unpaid vacation affect my hourly rate?

If you are not paid for some weeks, reduce the Weeks per Year value to reflect the number of weeks you are actually paid.

How do I convert monthly salary to hourly?

First convert the monthly salary to annual salary by multiplying it by 12, then divide by your annual working hours.

What is the difference between gross and net hourly pay?

Gross hourly pay is your pay before taxes and deductions. Net hourly pay is what remains after applicable taxes and deductions.
